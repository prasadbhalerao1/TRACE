# Problem Statement: AI Talent Intelligence & Recruitment Platform

## Theme

AI-Powered Talent Discovery, Verification & Recruitment Ecosystem

# Problem Overview

Traditional recruitment platforms primarily rely on resumes, keyword matching, and manual screening processes. This approach often fails to identify genuinely skilled candidates and creates inefficiencies for recruiters.

Thousands of students and developers participate in hackathons, build innovative projects, contribute to open source, and demonstrate strong technical capabilities, yet much of this valuable data remains unused in the hiring process.

Recruiters spend significant time filtering applications, validating skills, conducting initial assessments, and identifying suitable candidates. At the same time, candidates struggle to showcase their real abilities beyond traditional resumes.

There is a need for an intelligent platform that can transform technical achievements, project contributions, presentations, and hackathon performance into a trusted hiring ecosystem.

# Challenge Statement

Build an AI-powered Talent Intelligence and Recruitment Platform that enables organizations to discover, verify, evaluate, and hire candidates based on real skills, technical contributions, hackathon performance, presentations, and AI-driven assessments rather than relying solely on resumes.

The platform should create a seamless ecosystem connecting candidates, recruiters, communities, and hackathons into a unified hiring pipeline.

# Objectives

### For Candidates

* Create a verified digital talent identity.

* Showcase real technical capabilities.

* Receive personalized career recommendations.

* Improve visibility to recruiters.

### For Recruiters

* Reduce hiring time and screening effort.

* Discover hidden talent beyond resumes.

* Verify candidate skills automatically.

* Improve hiring quality using AI.
